## Geometry and conventions (step 5)

- Positions and normals go through `TDDeform` and `TDDeformNorm`, so transforms, non-uniform scale and instancing follow TouchDesigner's own handling. Ripples use `worldForNormals`, which excludes instance rotation (inherited from the core).
- Lighting is in world space: the builder sets the GLSL MAT's Lighting Space to World Space, and the shader passes world position, world normal and the vector to the camera to `TDLightingPBR`.
- The camera index comes from the vertex stage, as in the core. Orthographic cameras are handled.
- Environment direction is `uTDEnvLights[i].rotate * R`, the order a third-party TouchDesigner PBR shader uses; Derivative does not document it. The preview's rotation sign is an assumption.
- Output is premultiplied alpha with One / One Minus Source Alpha blending. `TDDither` is never called.

## Numerics

- Every `smoothstep` in the material, `envmap.frag`, `backdrop.frag` and the card has increasing edges. A unit test parses every call and fails on decreasing literal edges or the `(x, -x)` form. (Before the independent review this line wrongly said so already; see T5/M4.)
- GGX: N·H is clamped to 1 and the denominator kept above 1e-8. Without the clamp, float32 rounding gives N·H = 1.0000002 and d ≤ 0 near the peak at Highlight size 0.02 (M5). The visibility denominator is at least k² ≥ 1/64.
- The key light and half vectors are divided by `max(length, 1e-6)`, never normalized from zero (Key light direction 0,0,0, or a key along the view axis).
- Every shown stage, not only the final image, is capped at 1024 (M6). Test `outputs_finite_and_capped`: 18 renders into a 32-bit float target, no non-finite value, largest 1024.
- Roughness 0 takes one environment sample at level 0; above 0.001 it takes eight.
