# Name, credit and colours

The package name is `shaders-td`, displayed as **Shaders for TouchDesigner**.
Describe it as an independent toolkit with Maxime Heckel's published articles
as its knowledge base. Do not imply his authorship or endorsement. Put the
credit where a reader sees it, not only in legal files.

## The plugin's own colours

Icons and manifests use Swisoft's colours, not his:

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Ink | `#101416` |
| Paper | `#E8EAE5` |
| Accent (vermilion) | `#C4300A` |

The two icons in `assets/` are original drawings: contour lines for a field
and a three-node chain for a network.

## Tutorials and previews have no house style

A study belongs to the user. Do not give its tutorial or preview page the
plugin's colours, and never Maxime Heckel's palette or fonts: wearing his look
would suggest his authorship. Use what the user asks for. With no direction,
ask for a reference they like and something to avoid, or keep the host's plain
default.

His design system is in the reference library as source material
(`corpus/repositories/design-system/`), for a user who wants to study how he
builds themes.
